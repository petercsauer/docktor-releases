How it works

The Dock becomes a control surface.

docktor doesn't try to replace macOS. It sharpens the one place you already visit all day with previews, widgets, and quick info.

01

Hover

Preview every window for an app before you commit to switching.

02

Glance

Keep music, meetings, and weather beside the Dock.

03

Act

Click a preview, pause a track, or check the next event.

Dock Previews

Find the right window before you switch.

Hover an app and docktor turns the Dock into a live window chooser. Real thumbnails, one-click activation, and no need to open Mission Control.

ScreenCaptureKit thumbnails update in real time
Shows minimized and off-screen windows alongside visible ones
Click to focus the exact window you need

Window Switcher

Cmd+Tab, but you can actually see what you're switching to.

docktor replaces the stock Cmd+Tab switcher with live window thumbnails so you pick the right window on the first try.

Live thumbnails instead of just app icons
See every window, not just the frontmost one
Keyboard-driven — works exactly where muscle memory expects

Music

Control playback without leaving what you're doing.

The Music widget shows the current track next to the Dock and keeps transport controls one hover away. It works with Spotify and Apple Music.

Play, pause, and skip from the bar, or open the drawer for the queue and playlists
Works with Spotify (Web API) and Apple Music
Synced lyrics scroll in a second drawer when the track has them
Drawer pages remember where you left off

Calendar

See your next event before it starts.

The Calendar widget reads your Apple Calendar and shows the next event with a live countdown. The drawer holds the rest of the day.

Reads Apple Calendar through EventKit — no Google account
Countdown gets more urgent as the event approaches
Opens the meeting link when the countdown ends (optional)
Drawer shows your full schedule

Weather

Check conditions at a glance.

The Weather widget shows current conditions in the bar and a 12-hour forecast in the drawer, using data from Open-Meteo.

Temperature, feels-like, and condition in the bar
12-hour forecast with precipitation chance
Humidity, wind, UV index, and sunrise/sunset in the drawer
Save multiple locations or follow your current one

Release

What's new in 0.10.1

Public release 0.10.0 — docktor-0.10.0.dmg.

01

docktor is now free.

Every shipping feature — Dock previews, the Cmd+Tab window switcher, and the Music, Calendar, and Weather widgets — runs without a license or activation step. Existing beta license keys still validate and are recognized in the License pane.

02

Beta feedback widget.

Send feedback from inside the app; submissions queue locally when offline.

03

Spotify OAuth callback

uses `docktor://spotify-callback` for more reliable browser handoff.
