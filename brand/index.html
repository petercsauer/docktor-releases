Brand Identity

Resources for presenting the docktor brand consistently. Use these guidelines when referencing docktor in press, partnerships, or integrations. Give assets room to breathe — they should never feel cramped or cluttered.

Naming

docktor is always written in lowercase with a “k” — never “Docktor”, “DOCKTOR”, or “Doctor”. It is the brand name of both the company and the application.

When space is limited (social profiles, favicons), the app icon alone is acceptable.

Voice & Tone

We are

  • Concise and direct
  • Technical but approachable
  • Confident without arrogance
  • Focused on utility, not hype

We avoid

  • Buzzwords and empty superlatives
  • Overly casual or meme-driven language
  • Passive voice when active is clearer
  • Feature claims without substance

Visual Language

The docktor aesthetic draws from macOS system design — glass materials, subtle vibrancy, and layered transparency.

Glass & Blur

Panels use background blur (20px) with saturate(180%) to emulate macOS vibrancy materials.

Layered Transparency

Surfaces at 4% white, borders at 8% white — creating depth without harsh lines.

Rounded Corners

Cards use 12px radius. Buttons and badges use full rounded (pill). System-consistent corner radii throughout.

Usage Guidelines

Do

  • Give the icon generous whitespace
  • Use on solid dark or light backgrounds
  • Maintain aspect ratio when scaling
  • Use the full-color icon where possible

Don’t

  • Stretch, skew, or rotate the icon
  • Place on busy or clashing backgrounds
  • Add effects like drop shadows or strokes
  • Recreate or approximate the icon

About docktor

Short (one-liner): Glass widgets for the macOS Dock.

Medium: docktor parks music, calendar, a focus timer, weather, system stats, and live window previews right where your cursor already goes.

Full: docktor is a macOS utility that places floating glass widgets — music controls with synced lyrics, calendar glances via EventKit, weather from Open-Meteo, a Pomodoro timer, and system telemetry — beside the Dock. It also replaces Dock hover with live ScreenCaptureKit window thumbnails for instant window switching. Everything runs locally with no account required.
